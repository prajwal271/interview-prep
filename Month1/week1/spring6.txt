Each dice has 6 faces
Two dice rolled together

Total outcomes = 6 × 6 = 36

Dice 1 + Dice 2 = 7

(1, 6) → 1+6 = 7 ✅
(2, 5) → 2+5 = 7 ✅
(3, 4) → 3+4 = 7 ✅
(4, 3) → 4+3 = 7 ✅
(5, 2) → 5+2 = 7 ✅
(6, 1) → 6+1 = 7 ✅

Total favorable = 6

Probability = Favorable / Total
            = 6 / 36
            = 1/6

Answer: 1/6 ✅
